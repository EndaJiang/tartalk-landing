I. How we collect and use your personal information

We collect information you provide when using the services, and information generated through automated means while you use features or receive services, as follows:

1.1 Registration and sign-in

a. To register and sign in to TartTalk and related services, you create an account with a mobile phone number. The phone number is necessary to meet applicable real-name requirements. Without it, we cannot provide full service functionality.

b. The current version supports phone-number registration and sign-in only. If third-party account sign-in is enabled later, we will update this Policy and in-app notices before launch and, with your consent, may obtain public information from that platform (avatar, nickname, and other information you authorize) to bind to your TartTalk account.

c. Invite codes: During closed beta or certain campaigns, an invite code may be required before registration. We process the invite code you enter and the validation result to determine registration eligibility, establish the invitation relationship, and grant related benefits. So that you can resume an interrupted sign-up, the invite code may be temporarily stored on your device (see Section II of this Policy).

1.2 Completing your profile

When you first complete your profile, we collect your display name (you may use a nickname) and gender to create and display your account profile and to provide a more relevant experience. You may also choose to provide an avatar, date of birth, region, bio, custom ID, interest tags, and similar information. Except for information expressly marked as required, not providing optional information does not prevent basic use, but may limit personalization. These profile details are stored in association with your TartTalk user ID.

Note:&#8203; In Apple App Store privacy labels, “Name” refers to the display name / nickname you set in the app, not your legal name or ID-card name. The current version does not require a legal name for ordinary registration. If real-name verification is required by law, we will notify you separately in that scenario and obtain consent where required.

1.3 Real-person instant messaging and social features

a. Messaging: You may chat one-to-one or in groups and send text, images, voice, video, files, location, and other messages. To deliver messages, maintain history, and display them, message content, sender and recipient identifiers, and attachments are transmitted and stored through OpenIM.

Note:&#8203; In Apple App Store privacy labels, “Email or Text Messages” refers to in-app instant messages, used to provide messaging features. We do not read your device email inbox or carrier SMS inbox.

b. Friends and groups: We store your friend list, friend relationships, group membership, and group information. This information forms your social graph within TartTalk. The current version does not read or upload your device's system address book.

c. Moments: You may post Moments (friends-only / public plaza), browse, and interact through likes, comments, follows, and shares. We collect the text, images, or videos you post and related browsing and interaction records to provide social features and personalized recommendations.

d. Location messages and place tags: Only when you actively share a location in chat or add a place to a Moment do we, after system authorization, obtain precise coordinates, reverse-geocoding results, or coarse place information such as city or district. Location you choose to send or publish is stored in association with the relevant account, message, or Moment to provide location-related features. For content you publish with location, we may also use related interaction and usage signals for necessary analytics and to improve distribution and personalization. Coarse location is mainly used to display city/region place information. You may deny or withdraw location permission; doing so only affects location-related features.

1.4 AI character chat and interactive features

a. Using generative AI model technology, we provide AI chat and interaction (including chatting with system or user-created AI characters, character settings, and AI interactive games). To deliver these services, we may collect information you actively input (text, voice, or images) and edit or command data for analysis and computation so we can better understand your instructions and context and return more relevant content.

b. We record conversation history between you and AI characters (your inputs and AI replies) to support continuous interaction. You may clear conversation history in character settings.

c. Model improvement: Where permitted by law and relevant standards, after secure encryption, strict de-identification, and where re-identification of a specific individual is not possible, we may analyze and use such data—including to improve understanding of your inputs and the speed and quality of recognition and responses. If you do not want your inputs used for model training or optimization, contact us via Me → Feedback in the app or the channels in this Policy.

d. Game content: When you use interactive games, we collect player profiles, opening preferences, inputs, choices, session events, and saves you submit to run the game, save progress, and restore your experience. Some game content is produced by creators using platform tools and loaded as remote content; such content runs in a platform-controlled environment.

e. Creator Center: If you use creation tools to create and publish AI characters, interactive games, or other works, we collect the character settings, storylines, assets, and publishing configuration you submit for content generation, review, display, and operations management. Works you publish are registered on the platform as platform creation assets; see Article VIII of the Terms of Service and the Account Deletion Agreement for how they are handled.

f. Search records: When you search for users, AI characters, Moments, messages, or other in-app content, we process search terms and search interactions to return results, operate search, and improve personalized recommendations. We do not use this to collect browsing history from other apps or websites.

1.5 Voice messages, voice calls, and speech-to-text

a. Voice messages: When you actively record and send a voice message, the app accesses the microphone and the recording file is uploaded to our object storage and retained with the message record so recipients can play it and you can view message history. This is essential to message delivery.

b. Voice calls (not available in the current version)&#8203;: In-app voice calling has been discontinued in the current version; the entry point is no longer shown and the related SDK and native libraries are not distributed with the app, so no call audio or call connection records are collected, transmitted, or retained.

c. Speech-to-text (not available in the current version)&#8203;: Speech-to-text has been discontinued in the current version; the on-device recognition model is no longer bundled and the entry point is no longer shown. We do not request or use the iOS or Android system speech-recognition service, and we do not call any third-party cloud speech-recognition service.

d. We do not turn on the microphone in the background unless you are actively recording.

e. The current version does not provide system-level calling (VoIP / CallKit) and does not read or upload your phone call logs. If that capability is integrated later, we will update this Policy and system permission prompts before enabling it and obtain consent where required by law.

II. How we store information on your device

TartTalk is a mobile application. We do not use browser cookies or similar network-tracking technologies, and we do not set identifiers for the purpose of tracking you across apps or websites. To keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and speed up loading, the app stores necessary information locally on your device (together, “on-device storage”).

2.1 What we store on your device and why

| Category | Content | Storage method | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sign-in credentials | Access token, refresh token, IM session token, user ID | System secure storage (see 2.2) | Keep you signed in, avoid repeated sign-in, authenticate the messaging channel |
| Preferences | Language, theme and skins, notification settings, privacy settings, AI chat history toggle | Local key-value store inside the app sandbox | Remember your settings so they persist between sessions |
| Transactional flow data | Sign-up progress, invite code pending validation, pending push registration cleanup data | System secure storage | Keep sign-up and account flows continuous and secure |
| Cache | Image and media cache, conversation list and message summary cache, game asset cache | App sandbox directories | Speed up loading and reduce data usage |

The data above is stored only on your device. Through on-device storage we do not read other apps’ data, your personal files, or your photo library; reading your photo library requires your separate authorization (see the *TartTalk App Permission Usage Guide*).

2.2 Protecting sensitive information

Sensitive information such as sign-in credentials is encrypted using operating-system secure storage: on Android we use EncryptedSharedPreferences (keys protected by the system Keystore); on iOS we use the Keychain with accessibility set to “first unlock, this device only.” We do not store tokens or keys in plain text in ordinary configuration files.

2.3 Viewing and clearing on-device data

  • Cache: in Settings → Storage & Cache you can view cache usage and clear image and other caches;
  • Sign-in credentials: signing out clears the sign-in credentials and related data stored on your device;
  • All on-device data: uninstalling the app or clearing app data in system settings removes all information the app stored locally. For deletion of data held on our servers, see Section V of this Policy and the *TartTalk Account Deletion Agreement*.

Please note that after clearing on-device data you may need to sign in again, and cached content will need to be downloaded again.

IV. How we store and protect personal information

In accordance with law, personal information collected and generated in our domestic operations is stored in the People’s Republic of China. We do not currently transfer such information abroad. If we do, we will strictly comply with PRC law and regulatory policy and obtain required approvals or your consent.

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide TartTalk services. For example:

  • Phone number: Retained while you use phone-number registration/sign-in so we can provide service, handle inquiries and complaints, and protect account and system security.
  • Account profile, friend relationships, messages, media, Moments, AI and game content: Generally retained while your account remains active or until you actively delete the relevant content, as needed to provide ongoing features. Deletion scope and backup cleanup time may vary due to legal requirements and technical cycles.
  • Purchase and order records: Retained for the period needed to provide entitlements, restore purchases, conduct financial audits, and handle refunds or disputes.

After account deletion, active deletion of personal information, or expiry of the necessary period, we delete or anonymize personal information, except where:

  • Laws require retention; or
  • Extension is reasonably needed for finance, audit, dispute resolution, or similar purposes.

4.3 Security measures

a. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against improper use and unauthorized access, disclosure, use, alteration, damage, loss, or leakage.

b. We use encryption, anonymization, and other reasonably practicable means no less protective than industry peers, and security mechanisms against malicious attacks.

c. We maintain security management systems and data-security processes, strict access controls (authorized personnel only), and periodic security audits.

d. Despite reasonable measures and legal standards, internet security cannot be guaranteed 100% against every technical limit or malicious means. We will do our best to keep the personal information you provide secure.

e. We maintain incident-response plans. If a user-information security incident occurs, we will activate the plan promptly to contain impact. Where required by law, we will inform you of the basic facts and possible impact, measures taken or to be taken, steps you can take to mitigate risk, and remedies available. We will notify via push, email, letter, SMS, or similar means; where individual notice is difficult, we will publish an effective announcement.

To exercise rights under applicable law, contact us via the channels in this Policy. After verifying your identity, we will reply within fifteen (15) business days.

You may request account deletion via Settings → Account & Security → Delete account. The process works as follows:

a. 7-day cooling-off period: After you submit a deletion request, the account enters a 7-day cooling-off period. During this period the account is restricted but you can still sign in, and you may withdraw the request before the period ends. If you do not withdraw, the account enters an irreversible deletion process.

b. Pre-checks: We verify your identity, security status, and device information. If you are still the owner of any group, you must first transfer ownership or disband that group. If you have an active Apple auto-renewing subscription, the system will ask you to cancel auto-renewal first; otherwise Apple may continue charging after deletion.

c. Processing time: We process promptly and reply within 15 business days or any shorter period required by law.

d. Deletion is irreversible. After deletion we delete or anonymize related information, except where law requires otherwise. See the TartTalk Account Deletion Agreement for virtual entitlements, created content, and refunds.

5.5 Feedback and complaints

Contact us via Me → Feedback or the channels in this Policy. After identity verification, we will reply within 15 business days.

5.6 Cessation of operations

If we stop operating, we will stop collecting personal information, notify you individually or by announcement, and delete or anonymize personal information we hold.

VI. How we protect minors

6.1 General terms for minors

a. We take protection of minors’ personal information seriously. TartTalk is primarily for adults. If you are under 18, read and agree to this Policy with a parent or other guardian before using the Product and related services.

b. Persons under 14 may not use this app. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn we have collected personal information of a person under 14 without verifiable guardian consent, we will delete it as soon as practicable.

c. Minors aged 14 to under 18 should use the app with guardian consent and assistance. Guardians may exercise the rights in Section V on their behalf.

d. If you are a guardian and have questions about a minor’s personal information, contact us via the channels in this Policy.

6.2 Minor Mode

To protect minors, we may ask you to confirm age when registering or using TartTalk. You or your guardian may turn on Minor Mode at any time via Me → Minor Mode and set a 4-digit numeric passcode; several consecutive incorrect attempts temporarily lock the passcode, during which no action can be taken—please retry later. While Minor Mode is on, we surface content better suited to minors and top-ups, membership purchases, and other paid actions are unavailable, with some features disabled or restricted. See the *TartTalk Rules on Personal Information Protection for Minors* for details.

Note:&#8203; Minor Mode is turned on by the user or guardian themselves (self-declared mechanism). If you are a guardian and find that a minor is using the product without Minor Mode enabled, please help them enable it or stop using the product, and contact us via the channels in this Policy.
